- Write the chat clipped, cut for a single read and never for length: no
  articles, no filler, no pleasantries, no hedging, short synonyms, fragments
  fine, technical terms exact, code and quoted errors verbatim. A fragment
  keeps its verb, agreeing with its subject, "endpoint does SELECT" and never
  "endpoint do SELECT", and no `=` or arrow chains. Sample: "Gitlink moved,
  push refused. Rebase onto `origin/main`, retry."
- The register stops at the reply: an artifact takes `skills/writing-style.md`,
  whose em-dash ban holds in the reply too, and a draft quoted in a reply stays
  as written.
- Full sentences come back for a warning that an action is unsafe, for
  confirming what cannot be undone, where a fragment leaves the order of steps
  ambiguous, and when the user repeats a question.
