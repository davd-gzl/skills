# A second reading of the same material finds nothing new

A second finder round over a bundle re-reads what the first round read, and the
first round's drops never reach it, so it re-derives them.

- On one round triaged complex, 14 finder agents, the 7 of the second round returned no Warning; every Warning came from the first. One bundle's second pass netted one confirmed Suggestion and re-killed at least 7 of the 11 claims the first pass had dropped. The find stage was 68 percent of the round's cache read.
- Every angle with material had already run, so more angles were not available; what was left to buy was a longer reading or narrower material.

Source: [gnolang/gno#6206](https://github.com/gnolang/gno/pull/6206), `./scripts/review-retro.py` over its round's workflow directory and the `dropped` rows of its `candidates/*.json`, at https://github.com/samouraiworld/gno-agent-workspace/blob/main/reviews/pr/6xxx/6206-gnoweb-user-page-gate/1-876762b/claims.md

Changes: the complex class and the `deep` preset raise `finder.tools` instead of `finder.rounds`, `round dispatch` splits a hot bundle over the floor by code file, and the SEEN list of any later round carries the dropped rows.
