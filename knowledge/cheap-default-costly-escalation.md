# Cheap default, costly escalation

The plain word runs the cheap shape; a costlier shape takes a costlier word, chosen by how well the reader knows the code.

- The archived skill reviewed alone and added red, blue and correctness lenses on the word deep: 6 to 17 minutes and under 150k output for a solo round.
- The pipeline's plain word ran the 42-agent shape, 75 minutes and 1.1M output, before any escalation.
- Production tools price the cheap pass at a dollar or two and the verified pass at fifteen to twenty-five; vendor list prices on the survey's date, self-reported.

Source: the sessions on one machine, 2026-08 to 2026-09; the cost survey linked from `narrow-first-filter-after.md`.

Changes: `quick` for a change already trusted, `review` for the rest, `deep` for code that is complex or unknown, each a step up in agents and runs.
