# Finders, not votes

A second independent run of a finder raises recall; a second vote on the same claim from the same model family adds little.

- Nine same-family judges carried about two independent votes, and the best single judge beat the panel; one paper's setup, its own judge tasks. Source: [Nine Judges, Two Effective Votes](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.29800).
- Independent finder runs on separate contexts raised recall on hard bugs in the security-review sources the why table of `skills/README.md` cites, their own targets and scaffolds.
- Under `deep`, every angle the diff has material for runs twice and the candidates merge per line.
