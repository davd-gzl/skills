# Finders, not votes

A second independent run of a finder raises recall; a second vote on the same claim from the same model family adds little.

- Nine same-family judges carried about two independent votes, and the best single judge beat the panel.
- Independent finder runs on separate contexts raised recall on hard bugs in the security-review study the corpus cites.
- Under `deep`, every angle the diff has material for runs twice and the candidates merge per line.

Source: the studies cited in the README's why-table; `review-pipeline.json` `finder.repeat`.

Changes: deep buys finders; a vote stays at one; the repeat keys on yield once the outcome table has three rounds.
