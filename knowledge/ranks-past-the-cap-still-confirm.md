# Ranks past the cap still confirm

A finder's later candidates confirm at the rate of its first ones, so the cap is a cost knob and not a quality knob.

- At cap 12, ranks 7 to 12 confirmed at the rate of ranks 1 to 6; 51 of 55 candidates confirmed.
- At cap 3 every rank sat near 90 percent.
- The confirm rate itself separates little: one gate refuted 7 of 58 and confirmed 23 of 25 Nits.

Source: `review-retro.py --ranks` over two rounds, 2026-09.

Changes: size the cap on the outcome table, fixed against open, per angle, once three posted rounds carry one; until then keep it flat and low.
