# The chain sets the clock

A round's minutes are its serial chain, finders then the slowest verifier then the writer then the text pass; the agent count sets the queue behind it.

- One verifier ran 42 minutes and held four stages; a tool budget ended that.
- The runner holds 16 agents at once, so 148 verifiers are ten waves whatever the caps.
- Once the fan-out goes, the writer at 44 turns and the text pass at 41 are the pole.

Source: the baseline in `review-pipeline.json` and the retro, 2026-09.

Changes: budget the verifier, let a tool assemble the draft, put the text pass on the cheaper tier, and count waves before counting agents.
