# Two mechanical gates do not overlap

`round check` reads em-dashes, visible questions, finding headers without their
link and phrases pointing at the page. `prose-check.py` reads sentence length,
subject shape and process words. A draft that exited `round check` at 0 carried
36 hits under `prose-check.py`, 22 of them visible sentences past the thirty-word
ceiling, and the round's text pass had been skipped, so a hand-run pass spent
most of its budget on rewrites the writer could have made.
