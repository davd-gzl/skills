# Verify is the stage

A verifier per candidate with a fresh context and a worktree is where a round's tokens go.

- Standard shape: 30 of 42 agents are verifiers; the deep projection puts 75 percent of its cache read there.
- Batches verifying Nits refuted 2 of 33 for a third of one round's cache read; Nits now ship on the finder's read, marked unverified.
- A reflector that sees the diff alone and only removes is one agent; production reviewers use it as the precision step.

Source: `review-plan.py` projections and one round's retro, 2026-09; the OpenCodeReview paper for the reflector.

Changes: triage before verifying, a verifier per mechanism rather than per line, the run-per-claim kept for the high bands and for `deep`.
